Harborline: Ship Docking Sim

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Harborline is a single-player ship-handling game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game keeps

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • Your game progress (company name, fleet, cash and settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • The game has no advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • The game plays offline and does not send your data anywhere.

Purchases

The free app includes a one-time purchase that unlocks the full game. Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad) handles the purchase, under that store's own privacy policy. Harborline never sees your payment details.

To know whether the full game is unlocked, the app asks the store on your device whether your store account owns it. That answer stays on your device.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released.
